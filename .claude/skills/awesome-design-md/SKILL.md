# awesome-design-md

Source: https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md (MIT). Each folder in `design-md/<brand>/` holds a `DESIGN.md` describing that brand's design system.

How to use:
1. List brands: `ls design-md/`.
2. Pick 1–2 languages that fit the product (e.g. `apple` for product-page storytelling, `nike` for bold campaign typography, `starbucks` for warm retail, `airbnb` for soft friendly UI).
3. Read the chosen `DESIGN.md` and borrow its principles (scale, rhythm, component behaviour) — adapt colours and fonts to the project's own brand instead of copying them.
